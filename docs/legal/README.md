# Legal notices

These documents are the **default** legal notices shipped with EARTMP and are
surfaced in-app under **Help → Legal**. The single source of truth is
`src/presentation/screens/legal/legalContent.ts`; the markdown here mirrors it
for reference and review.

- [Terms & Conditions of Use](terms-and-conditions.md)
- [Disclaimer](disclaimer.md)
- [User Policies](user-policies.md)

> They are jurisdiction-neutral templates and **do not constitute legal advice**.
> Each institution should review and adapt them to its own policies and to the
> law that applies to it. Keep the in-app content and these files in sync when
> either changes.
